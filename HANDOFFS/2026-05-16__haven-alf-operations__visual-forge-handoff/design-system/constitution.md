# Quiet Operator — Aesthetic Constitution

**Project:** Haven ALF Operations
**Generated:** 2026-05-16

## Manifesto

Haven ALF Operations looks like a hybrid of Mercury and Stripe, translated through clinical. It feels quiet operator, composed, and intentional. Information is tight and ready. The interface leans on split by audience surfaces with Geist typography. The user should walk away feeling warm. Haven ALF Operations avoids Glassmorphism, Purple-to-orange gradient, AI-sparkle / magic-wand iconography.

## Allowed to feel

- Calm
- Restrained
- Quiet
- Considered
- Sober
- Authoritative
- Human
- Supportive
- Approachable
- Considerate

## NOT allowed to feel

- Generic SaaS template aesthetic
- Glassmorphism
- Inter on white
- Playful
- Whimsical
- Bouncy
- Toy-like
- Cold
- Sterile
- Aggressive

## The ten rules

1. Calm over decoration.
2. Hierarchy from weight and spacing, not color.
3. One accent color. Semantic colors restrained.
4. Critical states never rely on color alone.
5. Motion has purpose. Snap or glide; never bounce.
6. Tables and lists default to data density.
7. Numbers use tabular figures.
8. Critical identifiers (resident, patient, room) must never truncate with ellipsis. Wrap or scale down.
9. Dark backgrounds avoid pure black. Warm near-black sets the floor.
10. Money values and legal identifiers use tabular figures and never animate when they change.
