## Acceptance

1. A final matching source satisfies exactly its intended occurrence once; replay and concurrent delivery do not duplicate: probe (receipt with the source columns and the source author as recorder, occurrence completed, audit written; the same key, another key for the same version, and the service's own key all return the one ledger row; changed content under the same key conflicts; one receipt) and the race script (two deliveries of one version wait on the advisory lock and converge; a retry racing a re-delivery of the same version waits and converges without deadlock; a delivery racing a human recording in both orders leaves one receipt with the loser refused or pending; versions 1 and 2 racing in both orders leave one effective receipt at the live version).
2. Wrong period, site, subject, draft or invalid source cannot satisfy: probe (draft, voided-for-final, stale version, missing record, other site, unenrolled subject, non-allowlisted activity, wrong subject kind, off-period, invalid statement, unstated late entry, author outside the recorder list, deactivated author, unknown author, unknown reader field, broken reader, future performed instant: each a recorded refusal or pending row, none a receipt, no occurrence touched; a refused delivery never blocks the next; cancelled occurrences are never candidates; the other site and an aide without resident scope are denied before the reader runs and never converge on or read another site's or subject's rows; rows without a resolved subject keep no statement, author or native id).
3. Correction and void retain invalidation and needs-attention history: probe (version 2 supersedes the version-1 receipt as a chained correction with the corrected receipt verbatim and review reopened; a version after a human correction is a conflict; a void reverses the source receipt, returns the occurrence to pending/missed, keeps every prior receipt verbatim and leaves an `invalidated` row that only a reasoned dismiss closes; a void after a human correction is `source_not_effective` with attention; a void of a never-linked record is `nothing_to_invalidate`; re-recording after a void starts a new chain; a later version whose date moves to another occurrence invalidates the earlier receipt first and satisfies the other as a fresh chain, so one record never holds two effective receipts and its void finds exactly one) and the race script (a void racing a human correction in both orders: the second is refused naming the first).

Ambiguity is resolved only among predicate candidates (`select` of a non-candidate is refused; a stale revision conflicts naming the current one; reconcile replays; a settled row refuses further reconciliation; maintenance and the other site are denied). Every guard is proven: direct DML on allowlists, ledger, attempts and receipt source columns refused for session and service with and without the forged settings; the owner-run definer with the real token still cannot re-raise attention, move identity or resettle; migration-only registration; retired adapters refuse new deliveries; DELETE and TRUNCATE refused; a forced audit-insert failure leaves ledger, receipts and occurrences untouched; generic audit payloads hidden; the public RPCs are invokers.
