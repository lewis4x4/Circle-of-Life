## Walkthrough to execute when participants are identified

1. Verify the participant, facility, device, application revision and current permissions. Confirm all unresolved rules remain visible and no deadline is fabricated.
2. Under an approved requirement with unknown scheduling, record actual routine work. Verify automatic identity/time and no due or overdue judgment. An unapproved requirement must remain a draft.
3. Exercise a required-evidence activity: missing upload, retry, successful scoped finalization, current evidence completion and preserved original receipt state.
4. Have corporate open Facility → Activity → History and retrieve the permitted evidence. Verify another site's user cannot retrieve the record or file by identifier.
5. Report a problem without claiming performance, record a failed check separately, assign the approved next actor, and resolve/correct/retry with history preserved.
6. Review complete history/export, including confirmed-empty versus failed retrieval. Verify the source boundary and current access at download.
7. Record measured effort and retrieval time on the actual devices. Agree improvements against a measured baseline; do not invent a latency or time-saving target.
8. Record which old step is accepted for retirement and which remains required. Only the authorized operating owner records the outcome in COL-20/COL-21.
