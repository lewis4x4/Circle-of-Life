Explicit inclusive site-calendar interval1–366days; no future enddate. Reply must echo task, activity_key, facility, period,timezone and current subject/native availability. All27mapfields visible with truthfulgaps; bounded family reads fail503 when incomplete, nevertruncateandclaimzero. Historylatest100mustlabelhistory_completefalse. ## Native meaning and presentation

- census_daily_log: `log_date` identifies the snapshotday, created_at recordingtime. Schema007 has no final reviewer/approval field. Display occupancy/hold/available counts as native snapshot counts; never call them actual resident presence, balanced census or billable days. Missing dates are missing, notzero. Corrected sameUUID/day changes its contenthash/history; wrongscope/date/deletion invalidates the oldcitation.
- payment: payment_date is economicdate, invoice period_start/period_end is serviceperiod, created_at is recordingtime. Require authoritative immutable financecommandreceipt/allocation/sourceversion. No legacy payment upgrade; no payment=deposit, paidinfull, securedpromise, contractsignature or externalACK.
- trust: canonical accounts/transactions and periodmovement remain separate from legacyreviewrequired entries. Missingaccount is unknown, notzero. Do not total legacyandcanonical or imply bankreconciliation/historicalopeningbalance/maildelivery.
- finance_handoff: prepared/localapproval/invalidated/dispatchblocked are local states. No submitted/provideracknowledged label unless real attributable externalreceipt exists. Native currentversion/period/member/controlvalidity remains authoritative. No outbound action inHFOpanel.
