## 2. Vocabulary (inherited from COL-154 unless stated)

Final, version, recording act (`finalized_at` anchors the late and future rules for every version), version act (`version_recorded_by` is the source recorder), entry reason, readings (verbatim; satisfy only where an approved rule defines them), outcome, basis: as COL-154 §2. New:

- **Service record**: one inspection, cleaning or maintenance action performed on one occasion against the site (`facility-service`) or one named asset (`asset-service`), recorded final by a staff member who witnessed it or holds its certificate. `service_kind` decides the adapter, the activity and the required subject; it never changes after recording (a wrong kind is voided and re-recorded; F1 lesson from COL-154).
- **Performer** of a service: `staff` (a current staff member at the site, default the recorder) or `vendor` (a `vendors` row of the organisation linked to the site through `vendor_facilities`, exactly as 344 requires for a hand recording; `performer_label` optionally names the technician). A vendor-performed or other-staff service is always an on-behalf (or late) entry and needs an `entry_reason`; the command refuses up front (COL-154 policy 3k(iii)).
- **Stated next due** (`next_due_on`): the date the certificate or vendor states, kept on the record for the site to read. It is **not** a rule, not an occurrence, never copied to `facility_assets.next_service_due_at`, `facility_building_profiles.*_date` or anywhere else (acceptance 3: service recording does not silently advance unrelated lifecycle).
- **Certificate reference** (`certificate_document_id`): an existing, non-deleted, non-archived `facility_documents` row of the same site. The record points at it under the vault's own RLS and never creates, supersedes, archives or edits it.
- **Meal-level record**: a `meal_substitution` is identified by `service_date` and `meal_period` (the 237 vocabulary: breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack_am, snack_pm, snack_hs), optionally referencing the `meal_services` row for that site, date and period. Its `performed_at` must fall on `service_date` in the facility timezone, so the record satisfies only the period its meal belongs to. It carries no resident.

## 5. Commands (session; `haven.*` definer with `public.*_review` invoker wrappers granted to `authenticated`)

Order in every command as COL-154 §5: shape (22023) → actor and site (`haven.operation_source_record_actor`) → advisory lock on the request key, then on the record id → `FOR UPDATE` re-read and `deleted_at` re-check → replay by request key → record state → domain write under the token → token cleared → 346 delivery under the caller's authority with key `<key>:deliver` → request row → reply `{ record, delivery, linked, replayed }`.

- `record_facility_service_review(p_request_key, p_payload)` — `{ facility_id, service_kind, asset_id?, performed_at, performer_kind, performed_by?, vendor_id?, performer_label?, outcome, readings?, issue_summary?, next_due_on?, certificate_document_id?, note?, entry_reason? }`. Refusals by name: a facility kind with an asset (`A fire safety inspection is recorded against the site, not an asset`), an asset kind without one, asset not current at the site, asset type mismatch (`An extinguisher inspection is recorded against a fire extinguisher`, `A hood cleaning is recorded against a hood suppression system or kitchen equipment`, `An AC filter change is recorded against an AC unit`), a vendor not linked to the site (`Performer vendor is not linked to this site`), a staff performer not current, `performed_by` with a vendor or `vendor_id` with staff, future instant, late or non-self performer without `entry_reason`, `fail` without `issue_summary`, `next_due_on` not after the service date, a certificate that is not a current document of the site. `vendors.status` and `meal_services.status` are not consulted, exactly as the 344 hand recording does not consult them. Delivers `final` through `facility-service` or `asset-service` by kind.
- `correct_facility_service_review(p_id, p_request_key, p_expected_version, p_payload)` — `{ reason, asset_id?, performed_at?, performer_kind?, performed_by?, vendor_id?, performer_label?, outcome?, readings?, issue_summary?, next_due_on?, certificate_document_id?, note?, entry_reason? }`; `service_kind` is not editable; expected version as 347; voided refuses; the corrected content is re-checked against the original recording act; a correction by someone other than the staff performer is an on-behalf restatement (3k(ix)); a certificate is re-checked only when the correction restates it (archiving last year's certificate never freezes the record; review F1), whereas the vendor's site link is re-checked on every correction because the 344 statement rules re-check it at every 346 delivery, so a note-only correction of a vendor record whose link has ended is refused by name and the record stays at its version (re-link the vendor or restate the performer); a restated performer never inherits the earlier `entry_reason` unless the correction states one (review F3); an asset move within the kind is delivered and handled by 346 by source identity: it satisfies the target as a fresh chain, or lands as a visible `conflict` that leaves the earlier receipt effective (COL-154 3k(xi)); a correction whose delivery is `unmatched` while the earlier version still satisfies an occurrence is refused by name (`Corrected record no longer matches the occurrence it satisfied; void the record and record it again`) and the whole command rolls back, because 346 invalidates only when a candidate exists and the old occurrence would otherwise read completed on evidence the record no longer states (review F2; the void reverses with an attention row and the re-record lands unmatched and visible). The replaced observation correction applies the same three rules; drill-log corrections (347) are not changed by this segment.
- `void_facility_service_review(p_id, p_request_key, { reason })` — asset kinds refuse when the asset is retired, deleted or moved (3k(xii)); delivers `voided`.
- `record_dietary_record_review`, `correct_dietary_record_review`, `void_dietary_record_review` — same shape over `dietary_records`; `record_kind` is not editable; `meal_period` must be one of the six; `meal_service_id` (when given) must be the site's row for that date and period; `performed_at` must fall on `service_date` locally (`A meal substitution is recorded on its service date`); `failed` refused for a substitution or an approval (`A meal substitution is recorded as performed; state a problem as an issue summary`).
- The replaced `record_asset_observation` / `correct_asset_observation` accept the five kinds; AED kinds require an `aed` asset.

Authority: any managed actor with current site access may record, correct or void (as 347); whether the recording satisfies the occurrence is the governing rule's recorder list, applied by 346 to `version_recorded_by`. No dietary, maintenance or vendor role gate is invented (Q02/Q29 open).
