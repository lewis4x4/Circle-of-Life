## 1. Vocabulary

- **Correction**: a new performance receipt that supersedes the current effective performance receipt of one occurrence. It carries the corrected statement of the work in full (the same fields a recording carries), the correction reason, and the exact receipt it corrects. The corrected receipt stays verbatim; only its `superseded_by_receipt_id` and `superseded_at` are set, once.
- **Reversal**: a receipt of kind `reversal` that supersedes the current effective performance receipt without replacing it. The occurrence returns to unrecorded (`execution_state='none'`) and can be recorded again; the reversed chain stays readable history.
- **Chain**: the receipts of one act of work linked by `chain_id` (the first performance receipt's id). A correction extends the chain; a reversal ends it; a recording after a reversal starts a new chain. Finalized evidence counts for every receipt in its chain and for no receipt outside it.
- **Review**: the verification receipt (341) now bound to the exact performance receipt it reviewed (`verifies_receipt_id`, `verified_receipt_revision`) and to that receipt's requirement version. When the reviewed receipt is superseded, the review is superseded with it and the occurrence visibly awaits review again when the rule requires it.

## 2. Schema (migration 344)

### 2.1 `operation_execution_receipts` additions

| column | rule |
|---|---|
| chain_id uuid NOT NULL → receipts(id) | own id for a recording; the corrected receipt's chain_id for a correction or reversal; backfilled to `id`; immutable |
| corrects_receipt_id uuid → receipts(id) | the receipt this one supersedes; NOT NULL exactly for corrections and reversals; must be a performance receipt of the same occurrence |
| correction_reason text | 1–2000 characters; NOT NULL exactly when corrects_receipt_id is set |
| correction_seq int NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 | 0 for a recording; corrected.correction_seq + 1 otherwise |
| superseded_at timestamptz | set together with `superseded_by_receipt_id`, once, under the token |
| verifies_receipt_id uuid → receipts(id), verified_receipt_revision text (64 hex) | NOT NULL exactly for verification receipts; the performance receipt and its revision as reviewed |

`receipt_kind` gains `reversal`; `completion_state` gains `reversed`. CHECKs: a reversal has outcome NULL, performer `self`, entry `routine`, issue NULL, values `{}`, evidence_status `not_required`, missing_evidence `[]`, completion_state `reversed`, corrects_receipt_id and correction_reason NOT NULL; only a reversal is `reversed`. The 341 partial unique indexes keep their predicates (a reversal is never an effective performance receipt). Index on `chain_id`.

Guard (`haven.guard_operation_receipt` replaced in place, keeping the 343 body): on insert under the token, `chain_id` is derived server-side (never trusted from the insert) and `superseded_by_receipt_id`/`superseded_at` must be NULL; the only permitted updates are the 343 evidence transition and, under the token, `superseded_by_receipt_id` NULL → a receipt of the same occurrence together with `superseded_at` NULL → instant, once. Every other column stays immutable; no DELETE; no TRUNCATE. `revision` stays immutable (343 semantics): a correction therefore conflicts on identity, not on a moving fingerprint, and the superseded receipt is recognisable by `superseded_by_receipt_id`.

### 2.2 Evidence across a chain

`haven.operation_evidence_rule_met(p_receipt, p_rule)` replaced in place: it counts finalized evidence rows whose receipt shares `chain_id` with `p_receipt`. `haven.operation_receipt_evidence_unmet` and 343's satisfaction therefore work unchanged for the effective receipt of a chain; evidence rows keep their own `receipt_id`; the per-receipt byte dedupe stays. New evidence after a correction is prepared against the effective (corrected) receipt; 343 already refuses finalization onto a superseded receipt (`Evidence attaches to the effective performance receipt`) and on a stale revision, so an upload in flight during a correction conflicts and is re-prepared against the current receipt.

### 2.3 Audit

The 341 event-type check is widened in place with `corrected` and `reversed`. Every command writes its task and audit rows in one transaction; the probe proves a failing audit insert leaves the receipts and the occurrence untouched.

## 3. Commands (session; token; lock order as 341: work-authority lock with NULL roles → replay lookup → state checks → lock with the governing recorder list → validate → write under the token → re-lock → reply)

### 3.1 Shared statement validation

Extract the 341 statement rules (payload keys, performer, entry kind and reason, outcome, values against the governing inputs, note, issue, applicable evidence rules) into `haven.operation_work_statement(...)` returning one composite, with a reference instant parameter for the late rule. `haven.record_operation_work` is replaced in place to call it with `clock_timestamp()`; its behaviour must be byte-identical (the 341 probe is the proof, and a fresh replay of `review_hfo_execution_receipts.sql` must pass unchanged).

For a correction the reference instant is the chain root's `recorded_at` (the first recording of this work): performed earlier than fifteen minutes before that recording requires `late` with a reason; performed later than that recording plus two minutes is refused (`Corrected performed time cannot be after the original recording`). A non-routine entry without `entry_reason` takes the correction reason as its entry reason. The corrector is the new receipt's recorder; a corrector who is not the performer states the performer explicitly (`other_staff`, `vendor`, `unknown_historical`) exactly as a recording would.

### 3.2 `correct_operation_work_review(p_task uuid, p_request_key text, p_expected_receipt_id uuid, p_expected_receipt_revision text, p_payload jsonb)`

Payload: the record payload keys plus `reason` (required, 1–2000). Order: shape → lock (NULL) → managed occurrence (legacy refused) → replay by key (same recorder, task and content hash → the existing correction, `replayed: true`; different content → `This request was already saved with different content`) → the effective performance receipt must exist and equal `p_expected_receipt_id` with `revision = p_expected_receipt_revision`, otherwise `Receipt changed since it was read` (P0001, DETAIL `current_receipt_id=<id>;current_receipt_revision=<revision>`), which the route returns as a conflict naming the current receipt → occurrence not cancelled or removed → lock with the governing recorder list → statement validation → evidence: `missing` = applicable rules for the new outcome not met by the chain; `evidence_status` = `not_required` / `complete` / `missing`; state as 341 (`awaiting_verification` whenever the central version requires review, even if a review of the superseded receipt existed) → write under the token: new issue when the payload carries one (a failed outcome requires one, as 341); insert the correction (`chain_id`, `corrects_receipt_id`, `correction_reason`, `correction_seq`, request key and hash, fresh revision); set `superseded_by_receipt_id`/`superseded_at` on the corrected receipt; if the effective verification receipt verifies the corrected receipt, set its `superseded_by_receipt_id`/`superseded_at` to the correction too → occurrence projection: `effective_receipt_id`, `performed_at`, `execution_state`, `status` (`completed` only for `completed`, else `in_progress`), `signed_by`/`signed_at` = corrector/now, `verification_receipt_id`, `second_sign_by`, `second_signed_at` cleared when the review was superseded, `completed_at`/`verified_by`/`verified_at` = now/corrector when `completed` else NULL, `sla_met` from the corrected performed instant, `completion_notes` = note → audit `corrected` (event_data: receipt ids, chain, seq, reason, previous state, superseded review id, request key and hash) plus `completed` when the corrected state is completed → re-lock → reply `{ receipt, corrected, occurrence, issue, verification_superseded_receipt_id, replayed }`.

### 3.3 `reverse_operation_work_review(p_task uuid, p_request_key text, p_expected_receipt_id uuid, p_expected_receipt_revision text, p_payload jsonb)`

Payload `{ reason }`. Same locks, replay and conflict rule. Refuses when no effective performance receipt exists (`Occurrence has no recorded work`). Writes the reversal receipt (performed_at = recorded_at = now), supersedes the effective performance receipt and its effective verification, and returns the occurrence to unrecorded: `effective_receipt_id`, `verification_receipt_id`, `performed_at`, `completed_at`, `signed_*`, `second_sign*`, `verified_*`, `sla_met`, `completion_notes` NULL; `execution_state='none'`; `status` = `missed` when the grace end (else the deadline) has passed, otherwise `pending`; `started_at` untouched. Audit `reversed`. Reply `{ receipt, reversed, occurrence, verification_superseded_receipt_id, replayed }` where `receipt` is the reversal receipt and `reversed` the full superseded performance receipt row (the correction reply uses `corrected` for the same position and adds `issue`); a reversal reply carries no `issue` key. A later recording starts a new chain; the reversed chain's evidence counts for nothing; a reversal is never corrected or reversed: because a reversal is never an effective performance receipt, such an attempt is refused as `Occurrence has no recorded work` (no separate wording). The probe asserts the exact reply keys of both commands.

### 3.4 `verify_operation_work` replaced in place (343 body plus binding)

Payload keys `decision`, `note`, `receipt_id?`, `receipt_revision?`. When supplied they must equal the effective performance receipt's id and revision, otherwise `Receipt changed since it was read` with the current receipt in DETAIL. The verification is inserted with `verifies_receipt_id = perf.id`, `verified_receipt_revision = perf.revision`, and the audit `verified` event names both. Independence as 341 (the reviewer is neither the effective receipt's recorder nor its named performer). The route requires `receipt_id` and `receipt_revision`; the database tolerates their absence so the 341 probe keeps passing.

## 5. Verification

- Probe (rolls back; fixtures reuse the 341/343 shapes): correct with the right id and revision → new effective receipt, chain and seq, the corrected receipt byte-identical except `superseded_by_receipt_id`/`superseded_at` (column comparison), original performer, performed_at and recorded_at unchanged; a finalized photo on the original satisfies the correction (`evidence_status='complete'`, state completed); correction to `failed` requires and creates an issue; stale revision, wrong id and a second correction of a superseded receipt → conflict naming the current receipt; replay idempotent; non-recorder and other site denied before any write; performed after the original recording refused; earlier than fifteen minutes before it without `late` refused; review superseded on correction and `awaiting_verification` again when the rule requires it; verify with the old id or revision → conflict, with the current → success with `verifies_receipt_id` recorded; independence; reversal returns the occurrence to `none` with `pending` or `missed` by its deadline, re-record allowed with a new chain, old evidence not counted, reversed chain readable in history; reversal without recorded work refused; correction or reversal of a reversal refused; legacy writers refused on managed rows without partial save; a forced audit-insert failure leaves receipts and occurrence unchanged; DELETE and TRUNCATE denied; the other site sees no receipt; every earlier probe passes unchanged.
- Race script (three cases): two corrections with the same expected revision → one wins, the other conflicts naming the winner; a correction racing an evidence finalization → either the finalization lands first and the correction carries the evidence, or the correction lands first and the finalization conflicts; a correction racing a verification → the verification either binds to the pre-correction receipt and is superseded, or conflicts.
- Vitest: schemas, mapping (conflict with current receipt), routes (correct, reverse, verify binding required, receipts history, managed-row refusals in legacy routes).
- Gates: focused suite, typecheck, lint, native replay, strict gate `--segment COL-145-HFO-CORRECTIONS`.

## 6. Boundaries kept

No UI (COL-148). No substep or composite receipts. No reminder or urgency rule (Q29). No translation of legacy writers into receipts: they are refused for managed rows and untouched for legacy rows. Engineering policies to list in `OWNER-DECISIONS.md` (3g): (i) the governing recorder list may correct and reverse; (ii) a correction is a full restatement, not a patch; (iii) the late rule for a correction is anchored on the chain root's recording instant and a corrected performed time never follows it; (iv) evidence carries across a chain and never across a reversal; (v) a correction always reopens review when the rule requires review; (vi) a reversal returns the occurrence to `pending` or `missed` by its deadline; (vii) legacy writers refuse managed rows rather than being translated.
