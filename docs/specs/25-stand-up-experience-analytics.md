## Entry window (COL-350) and empty drafts (COL-298)

**Entry window.** When a facility may begin its Monday report is one integer per facility: `public.stand_up_facility_settings.entry_open_lead_minutes`, whole minutes before the Monday 8:45 a.m. America/New_York deadline. Null means the Haven default, `STAND_UP_DEFAULT_ENTRY_OPEN_LEAD_MINUTES = 1965`, which is Sunday 12:00 a.m. Eastern and is the behaviour Haven shipped. Bounds are 60 (Monday 7:45 a.m.) to 3,405 (Saturday 12:00 a.m.), so the window never reaches back into the prior Monday's meeting; a CHECK constraint and the TypeScript validator both hold them. The four choices offered to an owner are 3405, 1965, 885 (Sunday 6:00 p.m.) and 525 (Monday 12:00 a.m.).

The rule lives in exactly two implementations: `getStandUpEntryWindow` / `standUpEntryOpensAt` in `src/lib/stand-up/model.ts` for display, and `public.stand_up_entry_opens_at` for enforcement. `supabase/tests/review_stand_up_entry_window.sql` pins both to the same eight instants, including the November fall-back and the March spring-forward weekends. A lead is wall-clock minutes, not elapsed minutes, so Sunday 12:00 a.m. stays Sunday 12:00 a.m. across a daylight-saving change. `scripts/stand-up/worker.py` keeps its own copy of the default-lead rule on purpose: the corporate publisher must stay on the organization week whatever a facility is set to, and `test_worker.py` pins it to the same instants.

`haven.stand_up_save` reads the window once per save from server time and refuses a week that has not opened with SQLSTATE `P0409` and hint `stand_up_entry_not_open`, which the route returns as HTTP 409 with `code: "stand_up_entry_not_open"`. Browser time never decides whether a report may be saved. The workspace payload carries `entry_open_lead_minutes`, `open_week` and `entry_opens_at` per facility; `current_week` stays the organization default, so the all-facilities overview and the Front Office feed are unchanged.

Writing the window is owner and org_admin only, through the `set_entry_window` command, and again through RLS on the table. A facility administrator may read their own facility's window and may not change it.

**Empty drafts.** A `stand_up_reports` row exists only once a save carries at least one figure. A save with sixteen blanks on the open reporting period returns a receipt marked `not_started` and reserves no facility and Monday; the receipt is still written, so a retried idempotency key answers identically. A report whose sixteen values are all null reads `Not started` in the Haven overview, the sticky bar and the publisher feed, whatever `entry_origin` it carries. Historical corrections, imports, reversals and any save against an existing report are unchanged, as are CAS, idempotency keys, immutable revisions, receipts and the facility-and-Monday uniqueness rule.

**Unchanged by this work:** the 8:45 a.m. deadline, the 9:15 a.m. management call, the Monday-to-Sunday staffing period, the submission rule and the reminders to corporate.
