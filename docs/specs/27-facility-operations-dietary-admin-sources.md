## Every item has a path; none is silent

The settled coverage register (`col159-evidence/engineering-contracts.md` §1) gives each of the twenty-one items one of three dispositions. **Source**: a registered adapter delivers the record in the same transaction as its command — AED operation and equipment checks (`asset-observation`, widened), fire safety, fire and sprinkler inspections (`facility-service`), extinguisher inspection, hood cleaning and AC filter change (`asset-service`), emergency food supply check, meal substitution and dietitian menu approval (`dietary-record`); the COL-154 generator/CO and extinguisher-currency kinds are unchanged. **Human**: the person records the work through the 341 receipt path with COL-143 evidence, and the item's open question is named — marketing calls, referral and maintenance log updates, mail check and delivery, social media sent, company calendar, activity calendar posted, emergency management book update and licence renewal (Q28, Q14, Q04, Q07, Q25). **Never**: the review activities (sanitation log review AL-D11, drill coverage review AL-A07-01/02, generator/CO log review AL-A08-01/02) are never allowlisted for any source; a log cannot satisfy its own review. Migration 348 asserts that exactly its eleven rules exist, that the five COL-154 rules are untouched, and that no rule names a review or human-path activity.

## Three tables, four adapters, one discipline

`asset_observations` (347) gains `aed_operation_check` (→ `hfo-al-w04-01`) and `aed_equipment_check` (→ `hfo-al-w04-02`), each against an `aed` asset; the record and correct commands and the reader are replaced in place with the wider kind list, and "AED machine is working and all equipment current" is two records, never one.

`facility_service_records` (new) is one inspection, cleaning or maintenance action on one occasion: `service_kind` decides the adapter (site kinds `fire_safety_inspection` → `hfo-al-y02-01`, `fire_inspection` → `hfo-al-y04-01`, `sprinkler_inspection` → `hfo-al-y04-02` with no asset; asset kinds `extinguisher_inspection` → `hfo-al-y03-01` against a `fire_extinguisher`, `hood_cleaning` → `hfo-al-y05-01` against a `hood_suppression` or `kitchen_equipment` asset, `ac_filter_change` → `hfo-al-m11-01` against an `ac_unit`) and never changes after recording. The performer is a current staff member (default the recorder) or a `vendors` row of the organisation linked to the site through `vendor_facilities`, exactly as a hand recording requires (344); a vendor or other-staff service is an on-behalf (or late) entry with a reason, refused up front otherwise. `next_due_on` is the date the certificate or vendor stated and `certificate_document_id` points at a current vault document of the site: both are read-only record columns. A service record never writes `facility_assets` service dates or status, `facility_building_profiles` inspection dates, `facilities` licence columns, `facility_documents`, `maintenance_tickets` or `maintenance_task_completions` (the probe snapshots all of them). One vendor visit that covers fire and sprinkler is two records satisfying two components; an extinguisher inspection satisfies the yearly inspection and leaves the monthly currency check (`hfo-al-a07-03`) pending.

`dietary_records` (new) carries three kinds. A `meal_substitution` (→ `hfo-al-m08-01`) is meal-level: `service_date`, `meal_period` (the 237 vocabulary), planned and substitute item, reason, an optional reference to the site's `meal_services` row for that date and period, and an instant that must fall on the service date, so the record satisfies only its meal's period; per-resident substitutions stay on tray tickets and no resident is referenced anywhere. A `menu_approval` (→ `hfo-al-y01-01`) stores the menu label and the approver's label verbatim (no qualification is checked; no external person becomes a user) with an optional approval document reference. An `emergency_food_supply_check` (→ `hfo-al-m01-01`) carries typed readings and a pass/fail outcome; a failed check opens a `failed_result` issue that a later void leaves open. Substitutions and approvals are recorded as performed; a problem is an issue summary.

All three share the COL-154 discipline: final by command only, `record_version` bumped on every content change, corrections as a new version under the expected version and a reason (an asset move within the kind invalidates the first occurrence's receipt and satisfies the second as a fresh chain; a stale version conflicts naming the current one), void with a reason as a 344 reversal leaving an `invalidated` attention row, the COL-154 request ledger for replay, the domain token for every write, site-access RLS reads, audit, no delete, no truncate, and the COL-133 retired-subject boundary (an asset-kind record whose asset is retired, deleted or moved cannot be corrected or voided until a historical-record scope exists). Routes: `POST/GET /api/admin/operations/service-records`, `POST …/service-records/[id]` (correct | void), `POST/GET /api/admin/operations/dietary-records`, `POST …/dietary-records/[id]`; the observation routes accept the two AED kinds. No UI.
