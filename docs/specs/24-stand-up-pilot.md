**Rules settled 2026-09-21 and 2026-09-22 (COL-374, DEC-2026-09-21-03, DEC-2026-09-22-01; migration 555).** These supersede the 2026-09-15 wording where they differ. A bed **reserved** for someone moving in (bed status `hold`, or a `pending_admission` resident assigned) is not open; a bed **out of service** (maintenance, offline, temporarily blocked) **is** open and is also counted separately so the form says how many open beds cannot be sold today. Semi-private category is decided by **who holds or is reserved for the room's other beds now** — nobody: flexible; all women: female; all men: male; anything else (sex not recorded as male or female, a reservation with no resident, mixed) is left for the administrator and named on the form. "It was a female room because of who was in it last" is not a rule. The four bed figures are now **suggested from Haven's rooms and confirmed** exactly like census and hospital: `public.stand_up_roster_beds` computes them, `haven.stand_up_roster_keys()` lists all six compared figures, a differing figure needs a reason from the facility’s configured list, and the 457 commit-time guard covers them.
